# --screen-- concept

The paper has three numbered sections, then the references:

1. **I. Introduction**
2. **II. Related Works**
3. **III. Methodology**
4. **References**

At the defence the lecturer may point at any table or section. You need to know where everything is.

# --screen-- concept

The lecturer's 15 steps leave a trail in the paper. Steps 1 to 7:

- **Step 1, understand the topic:** Introduction, paragraphs 1 and 2.
- **Step 2, research questions:** Introduction, paragraph 5.
- **Steps 3 to 5, Consensus, study, CSV:** Related Works, opening paragraph.
- **Steps 6 and 7, AI and gaps:** 2.4 Cross-cutting Limitations.

# --screen-- concept

The paper follows the NJC format. Table captions go above tables (`TABLE I: ...`). Figure captions go below figures (`Figure 3.1: ...`). Tables have three horizontal lines and no vertical ones.

The abstract is blank because the lecturer asked for that. There are no Results or Conclusion sections yet.

The paper has no Results or Conclusion section. Why?

## --options--

- [ ] The lecturer asked for them to be left out
  > No. The lecturer asked for the abstract to be left blank. Results and Conclusion are not asked to be left out.

  > No. They cannot be written yet. There are no test results until the system exists.

## --why--

Results and Conclusion come after the system is built and tested.
