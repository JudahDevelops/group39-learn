# --description--

Give the card room inside and outside. Inside the `#complaint-form` rule, below `border-radius`, add `padding` set to `1rem` and `margin` set to `1rem 0`. The margin is `1rem` above and below, and none on the left and right.

# --hints--

Your `#complaint-form` should have a `padding` of `1rem` on all four sides.

```js
const cs = getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('#complaint-form'));
['Top', 'Right', 'Bottom', 'Left'].forEach((side) => assert.equal(cs['padding' + side], '16px'));
```

Your `#complaint-form` should have `1rem` of margin above and below.

```js
const cs = getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('#complaint-form'));
assert.equal(cs.marginTop, '16px');
assert.equal(cs.marginBottom, '16px');
```

Your `#complaint-form` should have no margin on the left and right.

```js
const cs = getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('#complaint-form'));
assert.equal(cs.marginLeft, '0px');
assert.equal(cs.marginRight, '0px');
```

# --help--

## --hint--

Padding is the space inside the border. Margin is the space outside it.

## --hint--

With two values, the first is for the top and bottom, and the second is for the left and right.

## --hint--

Type `padding: 1rem;` and `margin: 1rem 0;` on two lines below `border-radius: 8px;`.
