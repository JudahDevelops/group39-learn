# --description--

Form fields and buttons do not use the page font by default. Add a rule for `textarea, button` that sets `font-family` to `system-ui, sans-serif` and `font-size` to `1rem`. A `rem` is the browser's base text size.

# --hints--

Your `textarea` should have a `font-family` of `system-ui, sans-serif`.

```js
const family = getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('textarea')).fontFamily;
assert.include(family, 'system-ui');
assert.include(family, 'sans-serif');
```

Your `button` should have a `font-family` of `system-ui, sans-serif`.

```js
const family = getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('button')).fontFamily;
assert.include(family, 'system-ui');
assert.include(family, 'sans-serif');
```

Your `textarea` and `button` should have a `font-size` of `1rem`.

```js
['textarea', 'button'].forEach((sel) => assert.equal(getComputedStyle(document.querySelector(sel)).fontSize, '16px'));
```

# --help--

## --hint--

A comma in a selector lets one rule style two kinds of element. You used it for `h1, h2`.

## --hint--

The selector is `textarea, button`. The two declarations are `font-family` and `font-size`.

## --hint--

Type `textarea, button {`, then `font-family: system-ui, sans-serif;` and `font-size: 1rem;` on two lines, then `}`.
