# --hints--

Your page should have a third `h2` element, below the form.

```js
assert.lengthOf(document.querySelectorAll('body > h2'), 3);
```

Your new `h2` element needs a closing tag, `</h2>`, with a `/` just after the `<`.

```js
assert.lengthOf(code.match(/<\/h2\s*>/gi) ?? [], 3);
```

Your new `h2` element should come right below the `form` element, not inside it.

```js
assert.strictEqual(document.querySelector('body > form').nextElementSibling, document.querySelectorAll('body > h2')[2]);
```

Your new `h2` element should have the text `Your complaints`. Check the spelling and the capital letter.

```js
assert.equal(document.querySelectorAll('body > h2')[2]?.textContent.trim(), 'Your complaints');
```
