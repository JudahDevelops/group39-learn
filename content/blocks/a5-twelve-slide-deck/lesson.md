# --screen-- concept

The lecturer fixed the format of the deck:

> *Keep slides clean, visual, and concise. Avoid dense text walls.*

The deck is built: `Group39_IHMS_Defense_Deck.pptx`. It has 12 slides, and every slide has speaker notes.

# --screen-- concept

Slides 1 to 4:

- **1. Title & Members.** The project title, all members and the lecturer.
- **2. Introduction.** The setting and the three research questions.
- **3. Problem Statement.** What goes wrong today, with evidence.
- **4. Existing Systems.** What the reviewed systems do and don't do.

# --screen-- concept

Slides 5 to 8:

- **5. Proposed Improvement.** The value-add sentence, and today vs with the IHMS.
- **6. One Project Aim.** The aim, alone.
- **7. Three Objectives.** Design, implement, test.
- **8. Proposed System.** How a complaint moves through triage and escalation, plus the Jev option.

# --screen-- concept

Slides 9 to 12:

- **9. System Architecture.** The four-tier diagram.
- **10. Architecture Details.** What each tier does.
- **11. Technology Stack.** Each technology and why.
- **12. Conclusion & Impact.** What changes for students, wardens and Student Affairs.
