# --description--

Add three more columns after `level`.

- `fees_cleared` is a `boolean` (true or false). It must have a value, and it is `false` unless you say otherwise: `default false`.
- `unit` is text that may be empty. For a maintenance officer it holds the unit, such as plumbing or electrical.
- `hostel_id` is a whole number that may be empty. It must match a real hostel when it is there. For a hall warden it holds the hostel they run.

How a student's fees get cleared is one of six open design decisions. The placeholder is that an admin marks it. See <a href="/reference/decisions" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Group decisions</a>.
