# --description--

Under the heading, add a `p` element with the text `Water will be off in Hall A from 9 am to 12 pm on Friday.` A `p` element holds one paragraph of text.

# --hints--

Your page should have exactly one `p` element.

```js
assert.lengthOf(document.querySelectorAll('p'), 1);
```

Your `p` element should come after your `h1` element.

```js
const h1 = document.querySelector('h1');
const p = document.querySelector('p');
assert.isTrue(Boolean(h1.compareDocumentPosition(p) & Node.DOCUMENT_POSITION_FOLLOWING));
```

Your `p` element should have the text `Water will be off in Hall A from 9 am to 12 pm on Friday.`

```js
assert.equal(
  document.querySelector('p')?.textContent.trim(),
  'Water will be off in Hall A from 9 am to 12 pm on Friday.',
);
```

# --help--

## --hint--

A paragraph uses a different element from a heading. Its name is a single letter.

## --hint--

The opening tag is `<p>` and the closing tag is `</p>`. Put it on the line after the `h1` element.

## --hint--

Type `<p>Water will be off in Hall A from 9 am to 12 pm on Friday.</p>` on the empty line.
